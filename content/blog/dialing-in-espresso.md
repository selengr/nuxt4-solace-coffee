A quiet café still moves. By 8:30 the line is steady, milk pitchers are rotating, and the house espresso has already been tasted twice.

## What we track

We keep a short log for every shift:

- grind setting and dose
- yield time and weight
- tasting notes from the opening barista

If the shot tastes thin, we tighten the grind before we touch temperature. If it tastes heavy, we open the grind and confirm the scale.

## Why it matters

Guests may not see the clipboard, but they taste the result — a cup that stays consistent from the first pour to the afternoon lull.
