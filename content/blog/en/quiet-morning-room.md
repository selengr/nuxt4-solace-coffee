Solace is built for lingering, not rushing. That starts with the room, not the menu.

## Soft edges

We keep the counter short, the music low, and the brightest lights away from seating. People settle faster when the room does not shout.

## One path, clear views

A single aisle from door to bar reduces collisions. Window seats face out; communal tables stay deeper in the room for conversation that does not block pickup.

The goal is simple: a specialty café that feels like a pause, even on Mercer Street.
