A long menu slows everyone down. Ours stays short on purpose.

## Coffee first

Food should support the cup. Toast, granola, a soft egg sandwich — each plate has a clear job and a short prep list.

## Rotate with the market

Preserves change with fruit. Pastries rotate with our bakery partner. When something leaves the board, something better takes its place.

Fewer SKUs mean fresher prep and a calmer bar during the rush.
